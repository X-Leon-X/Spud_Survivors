## Ranked Weapons

Same weapon + same rank combines into the next rank. Max rank is R5.
Weapon ranks now count as class points: Rank I = 1, Rank II = 2, Rank III = 4, Rank IV = 8, Rank V = 16. This means merging keeps or improves weapon class bonuses instead of accidentally removing them.
Higher-rank weapons also use flat damage stats better: Rank I = x1.00, Rank II = x1.45, Rank III = x2.15, Rank IV = x3.20, Rank V = x4.85 stat scaling. This makes merging more useful in real builds where Ranged, Melee, and Elemental Damage matter.
Merged weapons are tuned to stay close to the combined damage of the two lower-rank copies while freeing a weapon slot. Separate copies can still be useful for wider targeting before slots are full.
If all 6 weapon slots are filled, shop weapon buys are disabled. Crate weapons can still be taken by replacing one equipped weapon from the crate reward panel.

## Current Balance Notes

- Wave progression should mostly increase enemy count and spawn rate.
- Enemy HP, damage, and speed now increase smoothly by small amounts each wave so later waves feel like upgraded versions of the same enemies.
- Big enemies should be slower, rarer, stronger, and drop more scrap.
- Small enemies should be faster, more common, weaker, and drop less scrap.
- Darters flash, then perform a short, very fast lunge.
- Orbiters are the planet-shaped enemies. They circle-strafe toward the player and move faster than normal chasers.
- Drummers are pink support blobs. They connect pink shaking buff lines to nearby affected enemies, giving them +25% speed and +10% damage.
- Crate recycling currently returns about 35% of item shop value, increased by Recycling Clamp.
- Each wave spawns a random tree count, always more than 2.
- Breaking a tree drops either a crate or healing fruit. Luck slightly improves crate chance.
- Healing fruit restores HP when touched by the player.
- Auto-fire targets enemies first. If no enemies are in range, it targets trees.
